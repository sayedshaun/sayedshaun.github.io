### BanSuite: A Unified Toolkit and Software Platform for Low-Resource NLP in Bangla
`2026` · The 19th Conference of the European Chapter of the Association for Computational Linguistics (ACL)

Md Abu Sayed, Faisal Ahamed Khan, Jannatul Ferdous Tuli, Nabeel Mohammed,
Mohammad Ruhul Amin, Mohammad Mamun Or Rashid

A comprehensive Bangla NLP platform integrating POS tagging, NER, shallow parsing, and
dependency parsing using a large manually-annotated treebank — achieving ≈90% F1/UAS and
outperforming existing Bangla NLP systems and multilingual LLMs.
